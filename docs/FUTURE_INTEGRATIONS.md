# Future integrations and verification

Current reputation is server-derived contribution usefulness. It is not lived experience, professional qualification, theological authority or spiritual maturity. Anonymous posts do not expose author reputation; no verified expert/church/creator badge is displayed.

src/future/contracts.ts separates a self-described, unverified LivedExperience from ContributorApplication and a server-backed ContributorVerificationProvider. Application states include submission, review, approval/rejection and revocation, but no backend/application UI/review service is implemented. Clients have no approval/role/payout setter. Before implementation: define reviewer identity and topic/credential scope, obtain consent, privately retain evidence under narrow RLS, validate credentials, audit decisions, support expiry/revocation/appeals, and prohibit paid/vote-derived verification. A type is not a working verification flow.

EntitlementProvider and organization/role contracts reserve future HisFullGlory+, Church Pro and creator boundaries. There is no billing client, webhook, payout, organization membership service or verified status writer. The six added capability flags are hard-disabled. Basic Bible/prayer/safety/support remain accessible. Future paid entitlements require authenticated server checks, legitimate provider events, idempotency, cancellation/refunds, legal approval and a separate explicit activation review. Existing giving remains external and independent.

GroundedStudyProvider accepts selected public Scripture and available source records only. Runtime AI is absent: backend credentials, source-ID validation, prompt/output safety, citation enforcement, honest unsupported questions and denomination-sensitive labeling must be implemented/tested before enabling it. Existing three checked context observations are not a broad historical or original-language dataset. Licensed translations/scholarship require legitimate rights and providers.

Circles v1 now has locally tested scoped roles/private applications in migration 006; hosted deployment and genuine Auth remain unverified (see CIRCLES_QA.md). Ownership transfer, privacy conversion, church/creator products and cross-Circle moderation remain future. Other future slices, not implemented: helper topic opt-ins/private notifications; mutually accepted Walk With Me/accountability/check-ins; recorded-day Prayer Chain; verified community insight imports; dedicated public Path/challenge/testimony share destinations; journey-based store recommendations. Existing public Praying counts do not assert continuous prayer. Existing shop links are preserved without sensitive struggle targeting. These slices need real consent/abuse/ownership tests, not static cards or seed activity.

For future HisFullGlory.com acquisition: link actual Bible/context/study routes, topical communities and existing devotionals; preserve explicit authority labels and privacy. Dedicated acquisition pages and Shopify/content publishing need later approval. No live site/store/DNS or external content was modified. Native push, compiled wrappers and device/App Store testing remain tooling/provider/release gates.
